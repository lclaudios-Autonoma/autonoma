02

Problem & Opportunity

  • The target audience faces an overload of demands, fragmented decision-making, difficulty prioritizing, and the lack of a single trusted point of support for everyday matters.
  • The opportunity lies in offering an assistant that doesn't just answer questions, but reduces mental load, executes actions, and maintains context across interactions — functioning as a companion, not a tool.
  • The addressable market benefits from low entry friction: a free plan for acquisition, affordable paid plans for conversion, and expansion potential across LATAM in subsequent phases.

10

Key Risks

  • Trial retention below 35%: gate at M4 — if below threshold, activate retention protocol (day 7 + day 10 touchpoints). Haiku 4.5 makes this period low-cost to sustain.
  • Trial → Noma conversion below 10%: benchmark is 15% (B2C SaaS median). Below 10% at M4 triggers pricing/onboarding review. The 14-day aggressive trial and single-plan model reduce friction.
  • API cost above R$6.00/user: Prompt Caching reduces cost by 90%. Cap of 120 Sonnet interactions/month + unlimited Haiku as fallback keep cost controlled.
  • Marketplace adoption below 15%: Onda 1 launches with 6 high-demand agents (one per core pain). If 30-day adoption is below 15%, product review and targeted notification strategy are triggered.
  • Privacy and trust: the product handles persistent personal memory — security, consent, and LGPD compliance are non-negotiable for retention and regulatory standing.
